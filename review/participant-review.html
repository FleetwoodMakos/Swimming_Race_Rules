Try it with three people

A short practical review with a newcomer, a parent and an experienced swimmer.

Status: not yet conducted. This is a facilitator’s pack, not a record of user feedback. Software and browser checks are reported separately.

Open the app · Allow about 15–20 minutes per person. Use a fresh browser profile or note existing saved progress; do not erase somebody else’s progress.

Before you start

  • Explain: “We are checking the app, not testing you. Say what you are thinking; you cannot fail this.”
  • Ask permission to take anonymous notes. Use participant A, B or C; avoid names and personal information. Record device and any access needs they volunteer.
  • Ask how confident they feel about racing rules from 1 to 5. Repeat afterwards and ask why; three people cannot establish a general success rate.
  • Give one task at a time. Let them try before helping. If stuck, ask what they expected; then offer the smallest useful hint and record it.

New swimmer or newcomer

Start without swimming knowledge. If testing with a young swimmer, have their parent or coach present.
